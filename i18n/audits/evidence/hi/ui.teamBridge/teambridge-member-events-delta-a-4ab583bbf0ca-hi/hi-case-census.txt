Why it matters for ui.teamBridge.events.others:
  five event sentences put {targets} in a postpositional slot — events.invited/removed/consented «{targets} को», events.declined/withdrawn «{targets} के लिए». When the list is shortened (3+ targets), {targets} ends in the events.others string, so that string must be oblique there and nominative in events.joined/left («{targets} बातचीत में शामिल»), where लोगों would be wrong. One ICU string cannot be both; the delta's new head noun makes the mismatch visible: the real render is «Dana ने Ava, Ben, और 3 अन्य लोग के लिए आमंत्रण अस्वीकार कर दिया».

Recorded so the choice is explicit: an invariant tail («{count} अन्य», the pre-delta form) is grammatical in both slots; a nominal tail needs the five postpositional lines restructured.

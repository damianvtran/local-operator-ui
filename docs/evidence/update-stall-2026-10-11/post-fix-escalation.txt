generated by the shipped function itself (esbuild over the module), spawned
exactly as `startInstaller` spawns it (`sh -c <script> lo-update-installer
<pid>`, detached, stdio ignored, env = inherited + plan.env), and pointed at
the SAME windowless Electron carrier the pre-fix repro used
(pre-fix-repro/carrier.js, Electron 44.3.0 from this worktree's node_modules).

## What these two runs establish

- Every stage is LOGGED with a timestamp: waiting, grace expiry, SIGTERM,
  second grace, SIGKILL, pid gone, exec. The pre-fix log had none of this, and
  the 26-minute stall left no line anywhere.
- The wait is BOUNDED in both directions: a target that exits on TERM puts the
  installer through at ~60 s (the first grace), and a target that ignores TERM
  still reaches the installer right after the ladder's last stage.
- `exec` semantics are unchanged: the process that ran the stub is the pid the
  spawner was given (recorded by the stub itself).

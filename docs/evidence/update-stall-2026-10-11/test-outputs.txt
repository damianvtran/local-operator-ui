## The harness this change ALSO moved, deliberately

    $ node scripts/owned-serve-lifecycle.test.mjs
    tests 34 / pass 34 / fail 0

`index quit preserves listeners, waits cleanup, bounds itself and exits nonzero
on failure` slices the quit block out of `src/main/index.ts` and runs it in a
`node:vm` context with every binding supplied. The new hard deadline made the
slice reference `quitExitDeadlineMs`/`createQuitExitDeadline`; the harness now
supplies the SHIPPED functions (bundled from `src/main/quit-exit-deadline.ts`)
and pins that the deadline the slice constructs is the shipped derivation over
the slice's own failsafe - one construction, tied numerically, so a literal
drifting on either side fails the case.

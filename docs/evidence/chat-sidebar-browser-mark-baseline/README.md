# The per-row browser mark, before it was removed

The BEFORE half of a deletion. The operator asked for the corner browser control on
every chats-sidebar row to go, and a deletion's evidence is the frames of what went:

> "I don't think we need a browser button for each chat row in local-operator-ui, can
> you remove that? Especially since we're going to have a pin icon that should show up
> on hover, it takes up too much space for a button that would be infrequently used."

**That reverses the ask this control was built for**, and the reversal is stated rather
than left implied: design R2 is titled "a corner affordance on every conversation row",
the commit that made it unconditional (`3996c3749`) records the operator's ask as "a
corner tab on EACH conversation", and `docs/design/sidebar-conversation-browser.md`
carries a superseded note naming both asks. The mark, its stories, its own
`docs/evidence/browser-conversation-mark/` set, its `renderer-driver` scene and its
contrast-contract rows are deleted with it.

## Why the before half had to be RE-captured, and what the headline delta therefore measures

**`origin/main`'s committed `chat-sidebar-status-feed` frames are STALE, and that is a
fact about `main` rather than about the story** (design round 1, D2; code review round 1,
R2 — the file said only "the committed frame was already drawn without a Globe", which
invites the reading that the space cost never applied here). The committed bytes were
last written by `8b4d1f0d2` (2026-09-16 17:12), and `3996c3749` — the change that made
the mark unconditional, so that even an EMPTY summary map draws a quiet Globe on every
row — landed at 20:23 the same day without re-capturing the set. Those two commits are
640 files apart, so the committed frames are pictures of an older tree as well as of an
older rule.

So a comparison against them is NOT a measurement of the mark, and the record says so:
with no Globe in either half, the difference between the committed bytes and this
branch's re-capture is the READOUT FIXTURE's removal — `Browser marks:` and the one line
of reflow below it — and the row-level subtraction lives in the pair that has the mark
on one side, `mark-on-every-row/` against `chat-sidebar-status-feed/completion-unseen/`
(sidebar half 2,991–17,366 px at >0/255, `dracula` 7,024; 425–646 px at >8/255, `dracula`
449). The staleness itself, measured rather than argued: this set's fresh capture of the
same story against the committed bytes reads 894–24,770 px at >0/255 and **384–634 px at
>8/255**, and in `localOperatorLight`, `sage` and `neon` the substantive difference's box
is exactly `14x78+332+433` — the three Globe glyphs, in the columns and rows the mark's
own slot occupies. That is the staleness: three glyphs the committed frames do not draw
and `main`'s code did.

## What these frames do and do not show

- **They show the control, its width and its badge, on a real row.** The Globe is at
  the end of every `mark-on-every-row` title, and `mark-populated` carries the
  count-and-badge cascade. What the same row looks like with it gone is the sibling
  set's frame for the same story, which is the after half above.
- **They do not show a `· binding` row or a current row WITH the mark**, and no frame
  can: those arrangements were drawn only by the mark's own deleted story specimen
  (`browser-conversation-mark--on-both-grounds`, `--trailing-statement`), whose
  `docs/evidence/browser-conversation-mark/` set went with the control. The
  `bound-row-current/` pair here is the honest substitute for the arrangement: it is
  the row shape the operator named, photographed on both trees, and it is unchanged.
- **They do show the width, and the pair that does is `truncating-title/`.** The
  reclamation this change is about is the **28px** the mark reserved on every title — a
  `size-6` control (24px) plus the wrapper's `gap-1` (4px), which the earlier prose in
  this file understated as "the 24px control plus its gap". At the 360px panel the
  evidence frames use, the title box goes **288px → 316px**; measured on the DOM by the
  design and QA streams independently, the row button's box goes **228 → 256px**, the
  slot **28 → 0**, and the truncating title's clip **135 → 163px**. In THIS pair the same
  claim is visible in pixels: the ellipsis at the end of the long title ends at **x 314**
  before and **x 339** after, in all twelve themes (`tokyoNight` reads 313 → 339, one
  pixel of antialiasing), and the mark's own glyph ink sits at **x 332–345** on all three
  rows of the before half — the columns the title's extra 28px is drawn from. The ink
  moves 25px rather than the box's 28 because the last glyph's ink is not the box edge;
  the box numbers above are the ones to quote.

  One honest qualification, because the pair is cross-tree: the two halves differ in the
  READOUT as well as the rows — the before half is the base tree's story, whose readout
  prints `Browser marks: none`, and the head's story no longer carries that fixture line.
  The row claim above is measured on the row. Whole-frame, the pair reads 5,957–21,271 px
  at >0/255 and 2,145–2,278 px at >8/255; the sidebar half alone reads 2,506–7,435 and
  543–602.

  **Every frame here, and every `chat-sidebar-status-feed` state re-captured beside it,
  was shot before `#346` (`fd6ec6086`) moved each palette's page ground, so the LAYOUT
  and width claim they carry is palette-invariant and stands while their APPEARANCE is
  superseded by `#346`.**
- **The themes are the twelve the sweep uses**, which is what makes these frames
  comparable with the sets they are read beside rather than with the fifty-nine the
  registry carries.

## The measurements, because "unchanged" is a claim until it is a number

**The metric, NAMED, because an unnamed one is what produced the figure this section
replaces** (code review round 1, R1; design round 1, D2; QA round 1, Q21 — three streams,
one number that reproduces for two individual themes and for no range of the set). Two
quantities, both pixel counts, both on the committed bytes:

```sh
# (1) anything that moved at all: any channel differing by more than 0/255
magick compare -metric AE <base>.webp <head>.webp null:
# (2) what SUBSTANTIVELY moved: the difference image cut at 8/255, written as the
#     PERCENTAGE because a bare `8/255` is not 8 of 255 here - measured, it leaves a
#     5/255 difference standing exactly as a threshold of 0 does (8 is read against the
#     65535 quantum), while `3.137%` and `7.9%` behave as 8/255 and 20/255
magick <base>.webp <head>.webp -compose difference -composite -threshold 3.137% \
  -format '%[fx:int(mean*w*h)]' info:
# and, for a region, crop to PNG first: re-encoding the crop as WebP adds noise of its
# own (measured: the same split through a WebP intermediate read 69,469 px against a
# whole-frame 48,515, i.e. 21,000 px of encoder noise)
```

**The noise floor, measured rather than assumed**: `bound-row-current`'s halves are the
same content on the two trees, and they read **172–2,196 px at >0/255 and 0–22 px at
>8/255**. So a >0/255 count carries this machine's cross-tree glyph rasterisation and a
>8/255 count does not, which is why both are quoted below.
| the three states the re-capture moved | >0/255 (12 themes) | >8/255 (12 themes) | sidebar half (>0/255) | readout half (>0/255) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `completion-unseen` | 38,880 (localOperatorLight) – 70,522 (obsidian) | 14,610 – 15,162 | 2,037 – 18,051 | 36,076 – 54,063 |
| `gate-answered` | 15,361 (obsidian) – 27,180 (sage) | 5,191 – 5,371 | 2,235 – 6,122 | 12,795 – 23,316 |
| `gate-parked` | 16,889 (localOperatorLight) – 30,207 (synth) | 5,780 – 6,079 | 1,215 – 6,028 | 14,010 – 24,731 |
| **union** | **15,361 – 70,522** (3.5–16.1% of a 780×560 frame) | **5,191 – 15,162** (1.2–3.5%) | — | — |

**What that says, and it is not what this file used to say.** Of the substantive
(>8/255) count, **25–238 px** falls in the sidebar and **5,149–15,094 px** in the
readout: the changed BLOCK is the readout's `Browser marks:` line and the one line of
reflow below it, and the rows are not in it — consistent with the staleness above, since
both halves of that comparison draw no Globe. The claim this file made — "the difference
image is the readout block and nothing else" — is true of the substance and false of the
bytes: at >0/255 the same image also carries the rasterisation the noise floor measures,
which is why `obsidian`'s difference box spans the frame (`773x560+7+0`) while
`dracula`'s starts below the top (`768x369+12+184`). The two figures the previous
generation of this file quoted — 18,366–48,659 px (4.2–11.1%) — are the `dracula` and
`localOperatorDark` frames of two DIFFERENT states, i.e. a range assembled from two
single themes rather than measured over the set; both numbers are real per-frame values
(`dracula`/`completion-unseen` = 48,659, `localOperatorDark`/`gate-answered` = 18,366)
and neither is a range.

- **`bound-row-current`: the pair is the same picture.** Re-captured on this branch,
  the twelve committed frames came back within **172–2,196 px** of their bytes
  (0.04–0.5% of a 436,800-px frame, worst on `radient`; 0–22 px at >8/255), and the before
  half here differs from the after half by at most that same **2,196 px** — the largest
  delta of the twelve. Nothing in this pair was re-committed: the committed bytes stand,
  because a jitter band is not a change.
- **`mark-on-every-row`: the after half moved by the readout fixture, not by the mark.**
  Its twelve frames changed by 15,361–70,522 px at >0/255 by state (the table above), and
  the substantive difference is the `Browser marks:` line and the lines below it, one
  line row higher. The rows themselves are unchanged on that pair — the committed frame
  was already drawn without a Globe — which is exactly why the BEFORE capture above,
  taken on `origin/main`'s own tree, is the only picture of the control this set could
  carry, and why the row-level subtraction is quoted from the CONTENT pair
  (`mark-on-every-row/` vs `chat-sidebar-status-feed/completion-unseen/`) and not from
  this one.

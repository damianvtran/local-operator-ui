WHAT EACH LINE IS EVIDENCE FOR
  * `[error] Update error: ...` and `[error] Error checking for updates: ...` are
    the two producers of one `update-error`, and `(silent mode: true)` above them
    is the background check that had no business reporting at all.
  * `err.message` carrying `net::ERR_*` is why the alert's whole text was the
    code: `update-error` sent that string and the surface painted it.
  * The intermittent codes across the morning, with the local server answering
    in the same second, are why the honest response is a bounded retry rather
    than a message - and why an unfetched feed must stay `unavailable` rather
    than being reported as "nothing newer".
  * The wrapped shape is why `transientTransportFragment` searches for the code
    inside the message (`src/shared/transport-failure.ts`), and why the doubled
    prefix is stripped at the source before the string is sent
    (`stripErrorPrefixes`).
